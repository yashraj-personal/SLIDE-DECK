03 / Security Research
🔴 Vulnerability Disclosure

Exposed: Unencrypted Lecture URL Leak

Discovered that Masai School's student portal exposed raw CDN video stream URLs in browser DevTools — without any token-based authentication or encryption — allowing paid content to be accessed freely.

🏴‍☠️ Piracy Risk

Any student could extract and freely share or mirror paid lecture content with raw HLS (.m3u8) stream links that remain playable indefinitely.

🖥️ Server Exposure

Direct CDN paths and internal server structure were exposed, creating a potential surface for scraping, DDoS, or content cost attacks on the provider's infrastructure.
